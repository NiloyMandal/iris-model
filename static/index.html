What do these measurements tell us?

Petal lengthThe strongest signal. Setosa petals are tiny (≈1.5 cm), Virginica's are long (≈5.5 cm).

Petal widthMoves in step with length — narrow for Setosa, broad for Virginica.

Sepal lengthGrows from Setosa to Virginica, but the species overlap considerably.

Sepal widthSetosa tends to have the widest sepals, an unusual exception to the trend.
